# 🧠 Navigating the Uncharted — Psychology Research Explorer

A visual psychology research project exploring **why people struggle with confidence, independence, and unfamiliar situations** — and the psychological mechanisms that may shape those responses.

The project brings together concepts from developmental, cognitive, personality, behavioral, and clinical psychology in an accessible visual format.

## 🔍 Topics Explored

- 🧠 Cognitive psychology & self-efficacy
- 👶 Early development and attachment
- 🧩 Cognitive distortions
- 👤 Personality and the Big Five
- 🪞 Self-esteem & internalized beliefs
- ⚖️ Novelty seeking vs. harm avoidance
- 😶 Fear of judgment & avoidance patterns
- 🩺 Clinical connections
- 🌱 Evidence-informed strategies for building confidence

## 📊 Visual Exploration

The website uses diagrams, charts, comparison models, and behavioral pathways to make psychological concepts easier to understand.

It explores connections such as:

**Early Experiences → Beliefs → Cognitive Patterns → Emotional Responses → Behaviour**

and how these patterns may influence a person's response to unfamiliar environments.

## 🎯 Purpose

I built this project to combine my interests in **psychology, human behaviour, research, data visualization, and web development**.

Rather than presenting psychology as walls of text, the goal was to turn interconnected psychological concepts into a structured visual learning experience.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

This project is intended for **educational and exploratory purposes only**. It does not provide psychological diagnosis, medical advice, or professional mental-health treatment.
